Our Story

COFFEE · TEA · MILK TEA · REFRESHERS

We start with young people who love coffee, quick conversations, and a place that feels easy to return to. SEVEN Coffee & Tea is imagined as a neighborhood stop where familiar drinks and small breaks fit naturally into everyday routines.

From the first morning cup to an afternoon milk tea or a cold refresher, the goal is simple: make ordering and enjoying a good drink feel effortless.

Our menu brings together coffee, tea, milk tea, refreshing blends, and add-ons so every customer can find a favorite and make it their own.

The story continues through the people behind every order. We value consistency, friendly service, and the little details that turn a simple pickup into a good break.

SEVEN is built around a youthful, energetic identity while keeping the experience clean, welcoming, and a little premium.

Whether it is a quiet study session, a catch-up with friends, or a quick reset between tasks, there is always room for a SEVEN.
