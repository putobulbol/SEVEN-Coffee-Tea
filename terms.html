CUSTOMER POLICY

Terms and Conditions

Effective date: October 20, 2026. These terms explain how SEVEN Coffee & Tea customer accounts and pickup orders work. By creating an account or placing an order, you agree to follow these terms.

1. Customer accounts

Provide accurate name, email address, contact number, and account details. New customers must verify their email using the six-digit code sent to their email address before logging in or placing an order. Keep your password and verification codes private. You can review your email and username and update your name or contact number from My Profile.

2. Menu, customization, and prices

Menu availability and prices can change. Your order total is based on the items, sizes, and add-ons shown at checkout. A medium drink is the default size unless you choose Large. Paid add-ons, including oat milk, are included in the displayed total.

3. GCash payment and verification

Pickup orders use GCash. Enter the correct 13-digit reference number and upload a clear payment screenshot. The store reviews the recipient, amount, reference, and transaction status before accepting an order. Sending payment details does not mean the order has already been verified. If the proof is rejected, review the reason and submit corrected information for the same order.

4. Pickup and order status

Pickup is available only when an order is marked Ready for Pick-Up. The only pickup timing choice is As soon as ready; the website does not offer scheduled pickup slots or delivery. Payment must be verified before the order is accepted. Follow the statuses shown in Track Order: Pending, Accepted, Preparing, Ready for Pick-Up, and Completed.

5. Cancellations and payment issues

Customers cannot cancel orders inside the website. If you need to ask for a cancellation or report a payment issue, contact the store as early as possible using the contact details to be confirmed by the owner. Orders that are already being prepared may not be cancellable. Refund rules and timing are still to be confirmed by the store owner. Do not send a second payment unless the store instructs you to do so.

6. Notifications

When available, in-site notifications may report that an order was submitted, payment was verified or rejected, or the preparation and pickup status changed. Check the website for updates; notifications may not appear if you are offline or the service is unavailable.

7. Privacy and payment proof

Information submitted through the website, including your profile details, email verification status, orders, and payment proof, is used to manage your customer account, verify payments, prepare pickup orders, and provide order updates. Use Track Order to view your current order and past orders under History. Payment screenshots may contain personal transaction information, so upload only the proof needed to verify your order and never share passwords or verification codes.

8. Website use and changes

Do not misuse the site, attempt to access another customer's information, or submit false payment details. We may update menu information, website features, or these terms as the service changes. The latest version published on this page applies to future use.
