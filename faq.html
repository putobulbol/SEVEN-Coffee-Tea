Order Cancellation

What should I do if my payment proof is rejected?

Open Track Order, select your current order, and review the rejection reason. Submit a corrected 13-digit GCash reference number and a clear payment screenshot for the same order. The order remains unaccepted until the payment is verified.

Rules on Ordering

Which payment methods can I use?

GCash is the only supported payment method. Enter the transaction's 13-digit reference number and upload a screenshot of the completed payment. The store must verify the payment before the order is accepted.

What do the order statuses mean?

Your order follows this sequence: Pending → Accepted → Preparing → Ready for Pick-Up → Completed. Pending means the store has not accepted the order yet. Acceptance follows payment verification. Preparing means the drink is being made, and Ready for Pick-Up means you can collect it.

Can I schedule a pickup time or request delivery?

The only pickup timing is As soon as ready. The site does not support scheduled pickup slots or delivery. Collect your order after it is marked Ready for Pick-Up.

Where can I view older orders?

Use the floating Track Order button and open the History tab. It lists completed, cancelled, and unresolved payment-rejected orders linked to your signed-in customer account.
